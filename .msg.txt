refactor(projection): fold the retirement probe into the workaround it probes

`lib/path-binding.js` had exactly one caller: the UPSTREAM-1 retirement probe in
`lib/patch-projection.js`. It exists to answer a question only that probe asks —
"does this schema READ `node:path.isAbsolute`, or did it capture our patched
predicate as a value?" — and keeping it in its own file meant a reader chasing
why `acceptsByReadingBinding` is not a plain accept-check had to jump files for
the mechanism.

The three predicates now sit at the bottom of `patch-projection.js` under a
section header that says what they are for. `BRIDGE` keeps its own doc block and
stays a `globalThis` symbol, because the module that INSTALLS the bridge is
`fs-shim.js`, and importing that would pull in `remote-fs.js` and the peer
resolution behind it — which a probe must not need.

`patch-subprocess.js` reads `BRIDGE` from `patch-projection.js` now, and
`projection-relax-smoke` imports it from there too.

The import graph is unchanged: `patch-projection.js` still only imports
`protocol.js`, so the five `fs-shim + patch-projection + path-binding` cycles the
plan warned about do not appear.
